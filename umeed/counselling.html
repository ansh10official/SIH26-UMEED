Questions people ask first

01 Will anyone at work find out?

No. Bookings are tied to your account, not your team. Counsellors keep their own private notes and only share information if there is an immediate risk to someone's safety.

02 How much does a session cost?

Sessions booked through UmeedBot are covered for you. If you need more than the covered sessions, your counsellor will talk through the options before anything is charged.

03 What if I don't click with my counsellor?

That's common and it's fine. Come back to this page and book someone else — you don't need to finish a set number of sessions first.

04 I'm not sure my problem is big enough.

There's no threshold. Sleep, workload, a rough patch at home — all of it is worth an hour. If you'd like a sense of where you're at first, the stress assessment takes five minutes.
